# Mission — GridBridge

## Product vision
A United States planning workbench where utility planners discover nearby construction projects, understand their milestone relationships and prepare evidence-based coordination conversations. Public evidence, Gemini extraction/briefing and MongoDB Atlas support every promoted result.

## Audience and geography
Primary users: transmission planners and construction/resource coordinators. Judges need a fast, inspectable example of the complete workflow.

- National vision: all 50 U.S. states plus DC. Alaska/Hawaii have explicit navigation; their screen insets never change actual coordinates.
- First regional lens: GA, SC, NC, FL, AL, MS, TN and KY. This is our product grouping, not an official planning boundary.
- First fully reviewed comparison: DESC and verified Georgia Power projects in GA/SC. Source availability and CEII review determine expansion.
- Canada, Mexico and U.S. territories require a later scope decision.

## Promise
Users see what is known, what changed and what needs review. Nationwide navigation is distinct from nationwide reviewed project coverage. The three-dimensional date view makes geography and milestone separation visible; height is time, not elevation. In-service dates do not prove construction windows.

## Non-negotiables
Strict sponsor center/haversine <25-mile rule; exact day gaps only for exact dates; unchanged golden fixture; public-only sources; no invented locations, owner identities, dates or costs. Atlas is the application database. Gemini performs visible product work. No application, cloud resource or domain is created by this specification revision.
