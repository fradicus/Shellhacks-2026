# F19 Time view

- Shipped: `/time`, a Three.js layer inside the MapLibre map. Located projects stand on their centers as light
  pillars topped by a bead at the filed in-service date; a glass sheet marks the analysis date; a year ruler gives the
  scale. Selecting a pair turns the camera side-on and draws a drafting dimension labeled with the stored day gap.
- "Play the story": a four-step guided flight (overview, top-ranked pair, widest-gap pair, the rule) with captions
  built only from stored facts; Esc or any manual pick stops it.
- 2D toggle, height slider (px per year), overview reset, Esc to clear, keyboard pair list, reduced-motion and
  WebGL/tile-failure fallbacks, phone layout.
- Honest by construction: stored distance and gap only; month/year dates as spans; unknown dates and unlocated
  projects counted in a "Not drawn" tray.
- Polish pass (#119): date-ordered sweep intro with a year counter and "N of M filed in service by then"; 25-mile
  circles around both centers of a selected pair, labeled on the far arc; halo and foot-glow sprites with depth fade;
  booth mode (25 s idle loops the story with a slow orbit, any input stops it); a sheet scrubber that ghosts later
  projects and relabels the sheet "As of"; a self-drawing loading mark.
- Measured in real Chrome at 1512×945 @2x: 57 fps during the sweep, 61 idle, 54 in a pair flight, 60 panning.
- Known gaps: no automated browser test for `/time` yet; frame rate measured on one Mac only.
