## Drive rule (C46)

- Pairs now follow C46's drive rule: straight-line candidates within 25 miles (inclusive) are the prefilter, and a stored driving route of 25 miles or less decides. Pairs carry `drive_mi` and a `route` summary, take their band from the drive, and rank on it (`national-drive-25mi-v1`).
- `python -m national_pairs --fetch-routes` fetches missing or stale routes through `matches.routes`, one request per second, saving every 100. The load and the website never route. A pair without a current route is excluded and counted in `route_states`.
- Measured: 1,352 straight-line candidates, 1,352 routes fetched (0 failures), 543 over 25 driving miles, 809 published pairs (2 confirmed, 807 tentative).
- The API returns `drive_mi` and the route summary. F30's loader tests supply a stored route for their synthetic pair (`[FIX-F30]`, merged first).
