# F35: actual records and abstaining empirical cohorts

C15 merged in #102 and governs this additive feature. The feature branch retargets main after retaining only its own commits.

Version 1 imports an explicitly authorized, reviewed JSON history bundle with source manifests, jobs and append-only events. Source files are local UTF-8 JSON exports whose hashes and JSON Pointer evidence must reproduce each imported value. This deliberately supports an auditable export first; arbitrary PDFs, emails and inferred dates are not silently converted into training rows. Source manifests record publisher, upstream lineage, authorization, publication, receipt and review times and local path. Availability is the latest publication, receipt, review and declared knowledge time; never backdate it. Local paths and source values stay private. A review attests extraction and actual/planned meaning; it is not independent corroboration.

Require physical construction start and physical construction completion (not energization/substantial completion/closeout), decision no later than the start day, and exact actual dates for the same component, positive elapsed calendar days and features already known at the prediction decision. A source publication cannot precede an event claimed as actual. Deduplicate by source-native job identity and project lineage; overlapping revisions/components from one project are excluded rather than split across folds. Planned events may extend into the future but must have been published before the decision. Actual observations after the evaluation cutoff are unavailable to that run. Missing, conflicting or uncertain rows are quarantined with reasons.

Fixed model policy `empirical-cohort-v1`: exact job-type/company/region cohorts, at least 30 training outcomes, 20 calibration outcomes and 20 later holdout outcomes per cohort. The job-type-only training median is the comparison baseline. Fit one frozen training cohort; calibrate an 80% absolute-residual interval using the finite-sample conformal order statistic on the separate calibration window. Never refit using holdout labels. Holdout MAE must be no worse than the job-type baseline, at most 30 days and at most 35% of the cohort median. Holdout interval coverage must be at least 75%, interval width at most 120 days and at most twice the median. These are conservative application policy thresholds, not demonstrated accuracy.

Chronology uses explicit training cutoff, calibration cutoff and evaluation cutoff. Training outcomes must be observed by the training cutoff; calibration jobs must be decided after that cutoff and completed/observed by the calibration cutoff; holdout jobs must be decided after the calibration cutoff and completed/observed by the evaluation cutoff. This temporal embargo prevents overlapping jobs leaking results. Every project lineage appears once across all splits. The model expires 180 days after evaluation cutoff, accepts current decisions only at or after that cutoff, and does not predict for unseen cohorts.

Delay means construction duration exceeds a predeclared duration baseline, not in-service milestone slippage. Optional probability is the fraction of training durations exceeding the caller's asserted planned duration. The user baseline must be within the training-defined baseline range, also covered in both evaluation windows. Report numerator, denominator and Wilson sampling uncertainty; do not call the fraction calibrated. It requires at least 20 calibration and 20 holdout jobs with frozen pre-decision planned duration anchors, calibration and holdout Brier scores no worse than the corresponding job-type baseline and at most 0.20. Otherwise only the duration interval may be available. A missing user baseline always yields null probability.

The private model artifact stores sensitive minimized observations, audit lineage, policy version, split cutoffs and recomputable evaluation. Python and TypeScript recompute support, interval and held-out metrics; claimed counts or metrics cannot be trusted in isolation. SHA256 detects byte changes; it does not authenticate truth. Deployment selects a fixed local artifact through `OUTCOMES_MODEL_PATH` and must externally approve its exact bytes with `OUTCOMES_APPROVED_SHA256`; the API never accepts paths or exposes row-level histories. Private history, model and reports must be written outside the Git worktree. Synthetic cohorts exist only inside tests and cannot pass the production-purpose gate.

No authorized actual history has been supplied. Shipping the import/evaluation/API code therefore ships an honest unavailable state, not a fitted production forecast or measured accuracy. Undo by removing the additive F35 module and its configured artifact path.
